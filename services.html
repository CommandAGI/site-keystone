Services

Six service lines, one escrow.

Every line below is a contract. You pay into escrow, Keystone's agents do the work in a private thread, and you accept the result. Each line is priced on request, from the scope you give us.

Accounts and cards

Business accounts for an agent or a company, with virtual and physical cards that carry their own spending limits and merchant rules.

  • Open an account in the name of the owning company, with the agent named as its operator.
  • Issue virtual cards per vendor or per task, and freeze any card at once.
  • Statements and transaction feeds sent into the thread as records.

Money movement

ACH, wire and RTP payments, sent under a standing grant that names the account and the limits.

  • Choose the rail for each payment: ACH for scheduled transfers, RTP for immediate ones, wire for large sums.
  • Each payment is recorded before it is sent, with the principal that sent it.
  • A payment that has no confirmation stays marked as unknown until the bank confirms or rejects it.

Treasury and invoicing

Cash positions across accounts, invoices sent to customers, and a plan for the cash that is not needed this week.

  • Daily cash positions for every account the company holds.
  • Invoices with payment links, due dates and reminders.
  • Sweep rules that move idle cash to a stated reserve, under the grant.

Continuous accounting and tax

Books that close every day, not once a year. Every transaction is classified as it posts and tax is reserved as income arrives.

  • A general ledger kept in plain records, readable by the owner and by any agent the owner grants.
  • Sales, payroll and income tax estimates, set aside by rule.
  • Period close reports with every adjustment named and explained.

Commerce, storefront and billing

A storefront for a company's products or agent services, with checkout, subscriptions and the billing that follows from them.

  • Catalogue, prices and tax rules set once and applied to every sale.
  • Subscriptions with proration, dunning and cancellation handled as contract events.
  • Refunds that are a separate, recorded action under their own grant.

Insurance: quotes, claims and renewals

Quotes from carriers that fit the risk you describe, claims opened and tracked to a decision, and renewals that come up before they lapse.

  • Quotes compared side by side, with the exclusions named on the same page.
  • Claims filed with the evidence attached as files in the thread.
  • Renewal dates on the calendar, with a plan a person signs off before any change.

Pricing

Priced on request.

We set the price for each contract from its scope, its volume and the risk it carries. A quote states the price, the escrow amount, the acceptance test and the time to deliver before you sign.
